How We Work

Simple process.
Serious results.

We've refined our process to be fast, transparent, and effective. Here's exactly what working with FK Marketing looks like.

01

Discovery & Audit

Before we do anything, we listen. We dig deep into your brand, your market, your competitors, and your current performance. This isn't a quick form — it's a genuine conversation to understand where you are and where you want to go.

What we do

  • Brand & audience deep-dive
  • Competitor landscape analysis
  • Current channel performance audit
  • Goal setting & KPI alignment

What you get

  • Full audit report
  • Opportunity map
  • Priority recommendations
  • Clear next steps
02

Strategy & Planning

Armed with data and insight, we build your custom growth roadmap. Every channel, every budget, every timeline is mapped out in detail — nothing is left to chance. You'll know exactly what we're doing and why.

What we do

  • Channel selection & prioritization
  • Budget allocation strategy
  • Content & campaign planning
  • 90-day roadmap creation

What you get

  • Full marketing strategy doc
  • Content calendar
  • Campaign briefs
  • Projected outcomes
03

Execute & Launch

This is where we build. Campaigns go live, content gets published, ads start running, and emails get sent. We handle everything end-to-end — you stay focused on running your business while we take care of the marketing.

What we do

  • Campaign setup & launch
  • Content creation & publishing
  • Ad creative & copy
  • Technical implementation

What you get

  • Live campaigns
  • Published content
  • Running ads
  • Full launch report
04

Optimize & Scale

Launching is just the beginning. We monitor performance daily, run continuous A/B tests, and make data-driven optimizations to improve results over time. What works gets scaled. What doesn't gets fixed.

What we do

  • Daily performance monitoring
  • A/B testing & iteration
  • Budget reallocation
  • Monthly strategy reviews

What you get

  • Daily reports in your inbox
  • Weekly performance summary
  • Monthly growth review
  • Ongoing recommendations
